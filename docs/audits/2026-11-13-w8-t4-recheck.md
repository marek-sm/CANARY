- Frozen corpus checked against: `corpus_hash` `5afb8e1ee8f120f7ea8abfcdf4870b1339cdc5f992fe19f8e50a56939b6c9859` and `source_ledger_hash` `dc4fde13c548b4bf1e9b455efc5cba951dc713a8e86423c0742174a9ff439c41` (`corpus/freeze.json`, #93). Six corpus checks pass:
  - the evaluation file names on disk, their `base_case_id`s, and the evaluation records listed in `corpus_files` are each exactly its 20 `base_case_ids`;

  - `corpus/candidates.jsonl`, which sets `pool_hash` and so the sample, hashes to its frozen value.
- `corpus/recheck.py` recomputes the sample with code that imports nothing else from `corpus/`, and it matches `corpus/select.py`'s committed recheck sample for the 20-base prefix (`tests/test_defense_freeze.py`).

## Limitation

All four sampled cases were opened and adapted by T2 and source-reviewed by the project lead under Section 5, so this recheck is the original reviewer rechecking their own sign-off. `SPEC.md` Section 5 step 8 says only that the recheck is independent. Decision 0032 item 6 reads that as excluding the opener and the adapter, so the recheck is permitted, but it is not a second person's check. The paper states it (decision 0032 item 6). The `W8-T2` fallback didn't fire, because T2's #93 merged before it, so the project lead rendered no fixture and no sampled case's recheck shares a renderer with its rechecker (decision 0032 item 5).
